Renumbering, and a Few Other Things

Since Saturday afternoon my weekly Claude Code limit had been used up, so work on the site stopped. The limit reset this Monday morning, and I split the rough draft I'd put together on Sunday into 04_1, 04_2, 04_3, and 04_4 and posted them. The old numbering was a run of three-digit slots from 001 to 999. Once I actually got to work, though, anything I wrote in one go felt heavy to me — heavy enough to put needless load on a reader — so I started splitting things up, and it became clear that a single piece will often end up as two posts, sometimes four, five, or more.

To handle that, I switched to ##_#. The base slots run from 01 to 99, and each slot has its own derived slots from 0 to 9. _0 is used when a piece stands on its own with no derived slots; when it's split, 1 through 9 are used. So nine is the most parts I can cut one idea into. Now and then an issue that heavy does come up.

What bothered me to the very end in today's 04_# posts was identity versus similarity. Yesterday's road and today's road are similar, not identical — yet a road map is usually consulted as-is for at least five years, sometimes decades, and causes no trouble. That's reference, of course. But there's a whole area of focused discussion about reference being mistaken for transformation: the hyperreal in Jean Baudrillard's Simulacra and Simulation. In League of Legends, the Scuttle Crab, the blue buff, the dragon, Baron, the minion waves, and the turrets of Summoner's Rift are hyperreal.

And it isn't just kids' play. The economy running on top of LoL is bigger than you'd think. Plenty of YouTubers make a living just playing it, plenty of PC bangs survive because of it, plenty of people became famous by winning prize money in its pro matches, and the livelihoods of the many staff who hold that system up are tied to it too.

The ruins and holy sites standing on countless religious and historical narratives, and the tourism, belief systems, and fundraising by organizations running on top of them, are structurally the same as LoL's hyperreal. They're strange territories where an identity that cannot exist is granted and put to use.

Anyway, identity and similarity turned out to be much less tractable than I expected, and I was still turning it over right up to writing and posting today. For now I've wrapped it up by working it in with the emphasis tuned to the flow of the text, but it remains one of the good, fundamental questions. Atman, Buddha-nature, self-nature, spirituality — the words vary, but they're all small offshoots of one demand: to perceive identity and similarity as separate things.

It's also a question that asks us to re-examine the implicit agreements and premises coiled at the core of most modern wars, massacres, conflicts, and threats — the sovereign state with territorial supremacy established through the Thirty Years' War and the Peace of Westphalia, or the nation of national self-determination.

The tangents just got written as I went. In any case, the numbering is fixed, and I've firmly put a "pursue token economy" item into Claude's global instructions. For now, the only option is to defend against AI providers' perverse games with the denominator.
