The Mind to Keep, the Mind to Choose

01Ceiling — attempts to move the maximum lifespan have begun, and proof will arrive late

Bs2FoB

Efforts to raise the maximum human lifespan have only recently become an active field of research, and they are now actually being tried on living people.

Opus 5.5

Lifespan research splits into two lines: raising the average, and raising the ceiling. Life expectancy in Korea went from 62.3 years in 1970 to 83.7 in 2024, a gain of 21.4 years in 54 years. That gain came from cutting the causes of early death, such as infectious disease, infant mortality and heart disease. More people now get close to the ceiling, but the ceiling itself has not moved. The longest verified life is Jeanne Calment's 122 years, ending in 1997, and in the 29 years since no one has passed it.

To raise the ceiling, aging itself has to be treated as the target. That direction started drawing capital only in the 2010s. Google founded Calico in 2013, and Altos Labs launched in 2022 with three billion dollars. Drugs that clear senescent cells, rapamycin and metformin are also being tried on people.

All of these attempts measure proxies, not lifespan: epigenetic clocks, inflammation markers, muscle strength. Proof that the ceiling has risen only comes when someone passes 122, so the results for people trying these things now will arrive decades later. Wait for certain proof and it will come too late. What's needed is the judgment to adopt, early, the treatments whose proxies show an effect and whose risk is low.

02Keys — the key that has reached humans is epigenetic reprogramming, and metformin is closing

Bs2FoB

Hyperbaric oxygen, metformin, approaches that use the epigenome — it feels like the keys are already being turned, slowly, one by one.

Opus 5.5

The three keys are not at the same stage.

KeyWhere it stands
Epigenetic reprogrammingER-100, injected into the eye to deliver three factors (OCT4·SOX2·KLF4) that erase a cell's marks of age, was cleared for clinical trials by the US FDA in January 2026, and the first patient was dosed on June 9. It is a phase 1 safety trial in up to 18 patients with glaucoma and optic neuropathy
Hyperbaric oxygenA 2020 Israeli study gave 35 people aged 64 and over 60 sessions across three months; telomeres lengthened and senescent cells fell. It was small, had no control group, and has not been independently replicated
MetforminObservational data on diabetics hinted at longer life, but in mouse lifespan tests it did nothing on its own. In a trial of older adults doing strength training it blunted muscle gain. The human aging trial TAME never properly started for lack of funding

The sense that keys are turning is true for reprogramming. This is the first time partial reprogramming has entered a human body. The thing to watch is whether it proves safe in the optic nerve and then spreads to other organs. Because metformin blunts the benefits of exercise, there is currently little basis for a healthy person to take it against aging.

03Goal — what to keep is the mind, and exercise is the means

Bs2FoB

It feels like the core of anti-aging is preserving cognitive ability rather than physical ability.

Opus 5.5

As a goal, yes. Muscles, joints and organs can be replaced once technology catches up. The brain is different. Whether the person after a replacement is the same as the one before is measured by whether memory, response patterns and self-reference carry over, and the brain is what holds all three. The brain is the ruler for identity, so it has to stay original the longest. If the mind has collapsed by the time ceiling-raising technology arrives, fixing the body leaves no one to carry forward.

As a means, the two can't be separated. In a randomized controlled trial of older adults, the group that did a year of aerobic exercise grew their hippocampus, the seat of memory, while the control group's shrank. Cardiorespiratory fitness and blood pressure are brain-vessel care. In 2024 the Lancet Commission on dementia reported that 14 modifiable risk factors play a part in nearly half of dementia, and physical inactivity is one of them. When strength goes, you fall, and a fall that strikes the head can damage the brain in one blow.

Physical ability is not a goal in itself but a means of protecting the brain. This is also why metformin counts as a weak key: a drug that blunts exercise blunts a means of protecting the brain.

04Frame — the frame of cognition is four cells: supply, structure, regulation, input

Bs2FoB

The split between physical and cognitive ability is too coarse a scale. Vascular health, keeping the brain's structure intact, keeping the hormone system stable — those are really the frame of cognition.

Opus 5.5

Those three are the frame, and adding one more fills the empty cell.

CellWhat it doesHow it fails
Supply: blood vesselscarries oxygen and glucosehigh blood pressure, atherosclerosis, high blood sugar
Structure: brain tissuemaintains neurons and synapses, clears wasteatrophy, inflammation, amyloid buildup
Regulation: hormoneskeeps the brain's working environment steadychronic excess cortisol, insulin resistance
Input: sensesbrings in the signals to processloss of hearing and sight

The fourth cell comes from the definition of a system. <Cognitive Axioms 03_1> defined a system as a frame that takes in, transforms and sends out. Cut the intake and the system shrinks even if the transforming part is intact. The Lancet Commission also lists hearing loss and vision loss among modifiable risk factors. That is why hearing aids and cataract surgery count as ways of preserving the mind.

Split this way, exercise and sleep turn out to be tools that work across several cells, not cells of their own. Exercise lowers blood pressure (supply), grows the hippocampus (structure) and raises insulin sensitivity (regulation). Sleep washes out waste (structure) and sets the rhythms of cortisol and growth hormone (regulation). How many cells a tool touches at once is the test for choosing it, and it is why exercise and sleep sit at the top of the list.

05Hawking — with output shut, the mind survives if the four cells hold, and output can be replaced by machines

Bs2FoB

Stephen Hawking is the classic case. He lost almost all his physical ability, but his mind stayed alive, and he was active right up until he died.

Opus 5.5

Hawking had amyotrophic lateral sclerosis. It picks off the motor neurons that move the muscles and leaves the four cells largely alone. Diagnosed at 21 in 1963 and told he was unlikely to live two more years, he kept researching and writing until he died at 76 in 2018. The table of four cells had input but no output. Hawking shows, with a whole life, that the mind runs intact even when output is nearly closed.

Hawking also swapped his output channel for a machine. After losing his voice to a tracheotomy in 1985 he spoke through a speech synthesizer, and in his last years a sensor read the movement of a cheek muscle to pick words. A machine took over the work of mouth and hands, and no one doubted he was the same man. It is a live case of the judgment that if the mind is intact, replacing parts of the body still carries on the same person.

Bs2FoB

Once organic robotics is advanced enough, Hawking is simply a healthy person.

Opus 5.5

Then the definition of health changes. We don't call a nearsighted person a patient, because glasses push the difference below the scale. Replace the output channel completely and motor neuron damage drops to the same place. Health stops meaning that the parts are original and starts meaning that the four cells are intact.

The shift has already begun. In 2024 a team at UC Davis implanted electrodes in the speech area of an ALS patient who had lost his voice, and turned the sentences he meant to say into speech with over 97 percent accuracy. The channel through which Hawking picked words one at a time with his cheek has moved to one that reads straight from the brain. The direct cause of death in this disease, respiratory failure, is also output-side movement, so if all output can be mechanized it becomes a disease in which you change the body and keep the mind.

06Implants — AI implants win on output first, and input comes later

Bs2FoB

One way or another AI will pass through wearables and be put into natural humans as implants, tools that extend cognition and reasoning.

Opus 5.5

The direction is right, and reading comes first. Reading signals out of the brain and writing signals into it differ enormously in difficulty.

The eyes and ears are high-bandwidth input channels. When glasses put results in your field of view, what the AI produces already reaches the brain fast. For an implant to beat wearables on input, it would have to write in the form of thought, bypassing the senses, and that technology is at the level of making points of light in the visual cortex. The narrow channel is output. Speech and fingers, the ways a person tells an AI what they want, are very slow next to the speed of thought, and reading intention from the brain has already reached humans, as the patient in the last section shows.

  1. Wearables: take in through the senses, send out through speech and hands
  2. Output implants: take in through the senses, send out straight from the brain
  3. Input-output implants: write straight into the brain and read straight out of it

At stage 3, AI walks the path mitochondria walked. Mitochondria were once free-living bacteria that took over energy conversion inside a cell, then handed most of their blueprint to the host's nucleus and became an organelle: an outside agent merged into part of its host.

07Leaders — once AI supplies HI, what sets leaders apart shifts to SI and PI

Bs2FoB

Political leaders in today's system are nearly all legal and administrative experts. They aren't required to have the domain knowledge — the HI — to properly grasp and handle matters in every field. But once natural humans extend their cognition with AI, leaders whose cognitive power actually matches their authority can appear.

Opus 5.5

The first six sections were about keeping the mind inside one person. When an extended mind meets power, the problem moves to society.

In the terms of the intelligence trichotomy in <Cognitive Axioms 02_3>: HI (hard, foundational intelligence) is the store of material a goal can draw on, SI (soft, selective intelligence) is the operation that filters out propositions with no information in them, and PI (performance, responsive intelligence) is the ability to take HI and SI in hand and draw out a contribution toward the goal.

Today the bureaucracy and staff act as a leader's outside HI, so leaders aren't required to hold HI in every field. Lawyers and administrators fill leadership because law is the operating language of the state, and the HI of its procedures is directly needed to exercise power. The price is that a leader's judgment is bound to the quality of the material the staff filter up.

With AI attached to their cognition, leaders can draw material from every field themselves, and power and cognitive reach can line up for the first time. At the same time, HI drops out as a way of telling people apart, because everyone has access to the same store.

The trichotomy has an asymmetry: when SI is low, contaminated material flows into PI, and if that repeats, HI itself corrodes. The material AI hands over is mixed with plausible errors. AI extension lifts a high-SI leader to the level their power demands, and magnifies a low-SI leader's misjudgments on the same scale. The leader's qualification shifts from domain knowledge to SI and PI.

08Selection — balanced candidates are filtered out in the primaries, not the general election

Bs2FoB

The worst outcome for Korea is a leader who, trapped in the one-sided view of Korea-centrism, clings to populism and propaganda to win and keep approval: populism and propaganda built on a biased anti-market approach that lacks any grasp of the perspective of humanity, the humanist perspective, a balanced view of history as a chain of geopolitical interactions, or the broad frame of a modern economy driven by markets, the division of labor and the technological innovation they bring, or on an approach that panders to memeplexes such as bloodline nationalism and collectivism. Yet for now it's clear that the system mostly puts that kind of person in the presidency. The more you stir exclusive group loyalties, the stronger your core support, and lock in just 5 percent of all voters as hard supporters and you have a big edge in the whole contest. A rational, balanced leader can't beat a rival who leans on that bias.

Opus 5.5

The structural judgment holds, and the place where the filtering happens narrows to the nomination stage. The general election is fought over the middle ground between the two camps, so in theory it favors the balanced candidate. But who reaches the general election is decided by primaries, with low turnout and heavy weight on party members. There the most committed decide the result, and the candidate who gathers them by stirring group feeling gets through. The balanced candidate is out before the general election, which becomes a contest between two nominees each picked by the committed.

The committed decide the general election too. Korea's presidential race has no runoff, so one vote more wins, and in 2022 the gap was 0.73 percentage points. In an even race, the turnout of the committed who always show up settles it. The share of committed voters needed depends on turnout and on how the camps line up, so the 5 percent figure can't be checked, but the structure in which a small share sways the whole contest holds.

The selection machinery doesn't measure SI and PI; it measures the power to mobilize. Even if AI extension multiplies capable people, as long as the filter stays the same they get filtered out. The bottleneck is not the supply of ability but the structure of selection.

09Representation — selection has drifted toward measuring mobilization, and the job toward long judgments

Bs2FoB

This is where the modern representative administrative state is already starting to show its limits.

Opus 5.5

The limits come from the collapse of the conditions representative government assumed when it was designed.

Assumed at designNow
Information is slow and scarce, so voters hand judgment to representativesInformation floods in, and recommendation algorithms spread whatever provokes anger
The press and parties filter informationFilters can be bypassed to mobilize the committed directly, and the cost of mobilizing has collapsed
A generalist representative can grasp the policy areasThe areas have specialized, and real judgment has moved to bureaucrats and courts
Election cycles match the time scale of problemsProblems that play out over decades go unrewarded in four- or five-year cycles

The core is that two directions have pulled apart. Selection has tilted toward measuring mobilization, while the job has moved toward complex judgments over long time scales. Korea's total fertility rate falling to 0.72 in 2023 is what builds up when a problem can't be handled on an electoral cycle.

There are experiments in choosing deciders by something other than mobilization. In 2016 Ireland formed a Citizens' Assembly of 99 randomly selected citizens, and their deliberation, after hearing from experts, led to the 2018 constitutional referendum. Sortition is a device that leaves no room for the power to mobilize. Whether it could replace the administrative state as a whole has not been tested.

10Pandora — representative government draws its legitimacy from consent and removal, not competence

Bs2FoB

This is a very hard problem. It's Pandora's box.

Opus 5.5

Open the box and even the functions representative government holds up today begin to shake. As Popper pointed out, it is weak as a device for choosing the most capable and strong as a device for removing rulers without bloodshed. Its legitimacy comes from consent, not competence. It pays the cost of sometimes electing incompetent leaders, and in return hands every voter the means of removal.

To switch the selection standard to competence, someone has to set and apply the scale for SI and PI, and who checks those measurers becomes the problem at once. Regimes that selected by competence have historically slid toward the measurers seizing power. The moment a competence standard comes in, consent as the source of legitimacy and the means of removal both begin to shake.

AI extension makes this worse. A new question arises: can voters who aren't extended evaluate the judgment of a leader who is? No one has yet offered a design that brings competence into selection while keeping removal grounded in consent.

11Free mandate — suspending thought about the whole of society is higher-order computation, and handing over the removal switch turns suspension into dispossession

Bs2FoB

So in the end, shouldn't it converge on some variant of Plato's rule by the wise? Suspension is higher-order computation. Members of the social body, who live keeping their focus on their own everyday concerns, focus their cognition on their own concerns and suspend cognition about the problems of society as a whole. That suspension is the higher-order computation, and what fills the gap it leaves is the election of representatives and the free mandate.

Opus 5.5

Suspending is not declining to compute; it is a computation one level up that decides where computation goes. A person's cognition is limited, and trying to compute everything leaves both their own concerns and society as a whole handled coarsely. The judgment to set down the problems of society and gather one's computation on one's own life is the higher-order computation. The free mandate Burke set out in his 1774 Bristol speech is the device that fills that gap. The representative is not bound by the voters' instructions but computes by their own judgment, taking on the share the voters suspended.

Where this parts from Plato is not whether the work is divided but who holds the removal switch.

ComputationSelectionRemoval
Platothe wise carry itthe guardians raise and choose themnone
Free mandaterepresentatives carry itvoters choose themvoters hold it

A suspension is only a suspension if it can be taken back. Under the free mandate, voters suspend the process of computation but never let go of the switch that replaces a representative on the results. Just as a patient chooses a doctor by the results of treatment without medical knowledge, using the switch needs only the ability to judge results, not the process. A voter who has gathered their computation on their own concerns can still hold the switch, and this also resolves the question from the last section about voters who aren't extended. Plato hands the switch to the wise along with the computation. Since it can't be taken back, it isn't suspension but dispossession.

So there is no convergence on Plato. What's needed is to keep the frame of the free mandate and change only the filter at the entrance: change how primaries and nominations measure, so that SI and PI are caught instead of the power to mobilize. Move the switch as well and suspension turns into dispossession, and the box opens. The weakness that remains is problems like population decline whose results appear decades later. A switch that judges by results doesn't fire in time.

12Two mandates — a technical mandate hands over verifiable answers, a political mandate hands over judgments that bind everyone

Bs2FoB

Someone who only needs to drive a car suspends cognition about the specific technology, engineering structure and mechanics that go into building it. That too is the higher-order computation of suspension, with the extra computation delegated to engineers and experts. But it's a little different from electing representatives and the free mandate in politics.

Opus 5.5

Both are suspension and delegation, but they differ in what is handed over and how it's taken back.

Technical mandate (a car)Political mandate (a representative)
What's handed overcomputation with set answers, where physics and safety standards sort right from wrongjudgment mixed with values, where what comes first is decided outside the calculation
Verificationother experts reproduce and check it against the same standardsthere is no outside standard to check the result against
Seeing resultsthe individual meets them at once: the car runs or it stopsthey show up late, collectively, with tangled causes
Switcheach holds their own and can buy another car at any timeeveryone holds one switch together and presses it only at elections
Bindingonly the buyer rides in the carthose who voted against are bound by the result too

In Hirschman's terms, the switch of a technical mandate is exit and the switch of a political mandate is voice. If you don't like the car, you leave for another on your own. If you don't like the government, you can't leave on your own; you have to change it together with a majority.

This difference separates the legitimacy of the two mandates. A technical mandate hands over answers that can be checked, so confirming competence is enough; that's why licenses and qualifying exams work as entrance filters. A political mandate hands over judgments that bind everyone, so competence alone can't ground it, and it needs the consent of those who are bound. The measurer problem from section 10 comes from here: value judgments have no answer key you can hold up like an exam.

Some posts sit between the two. A constitutional justice takes on both a checkable competence, legal expertise, and value judgments that bind everyone. So the post mixes the technical mandate's filter, formal qualifications, with the political mandate's consent, appointment by elected power.

13Constitutional Court — a justice's removal switch is set to a term, not held by a person

Bs2FoB

Constitutional Court justices do come close to a free mandate, chosen on the basis of expertise in a somewhat specialized area of computation. But the power to replace them is held not by voters but by the president and the National Assembly that voters elect.

Opus 5.5

What the president and the Assembly hold is the power of appointment; removal is left to the term.

  • Entrance filter: at least 15 years in the legal profession, and at least 40 years old
  • Selection: the president appoints all nine; the Assembly elects three, the Chief Justice of the Supreme Court nominates three, and the president picks three directly
  • Removal: under Article 112 of the Constitution, a justice can't be removed except by impeachment or a sentence of imprisonment. Mid-term, not even the appointing side can replace them; replacement happens when the six-year term ends

Voter consent is passed along one step removed, and the removal switch is set to time rather than held by a person. The design keeps mobilized politics from swapping justices out at will. The four-year term of the Governor of the Bank of Korea follows the same design. Society already sets apart, like this, the domains where mobilization would be dangerous.

This structure is a working case of what section 11 described: keep the frame and change the entrance filter. It has two limits. The entrance filter measures legal HI, not SI or PI. And since the appointers were chosen by mobilization, the filter problem from the earlier stage carries straight over. That is why appointing justices becomes a partisan fight.

14Trial by public opinion — the rule of law fixes the scale first, and trial by opinion changes it after seeing the target

Bs2FoB

From a legal scholar's standpoint, trial by public opinion is the collapse of the rule of law.

Opus 5.5

The rule of law sets its rules before any act and measures by the same rules whoever the actor. The result can be known in advance, and the same act gets the same verdict. In the frame of <Cognitive Axioms 04_3>, law is a scale fixed before measurement, and equality before the law is putting the same scale to everyone.

Trial by public opinion looks at the case and the person first, and the mood of the moment decides the verdict. Because the standard is set after the act, it works like retroactive law and predictability collapses; because who you are outweighs what you did, equality collapses.

Insulating justices with fixed terms was a design to keep mobilization's hands off this scale. Trial by opinion is mobilization's pressure breaking through that insulation into the domain of judgment. The force that captured the selection filter has spread into the courts.

15Guardian of the constitution — Kelsen insulated the exit, and what is failing now is the entrance

Bs2FoB

The rule of law is the structural skeleton of modern humanist politics, and representative government is added under it. But electing representatives by the power to mobilize, and a judiciary appointed by those representatives, do a lot to encourage trial by public opinion. It's a hard problem. There's the debate over the guardian of the constitution.

Opus 5.5

The guardian debate took on this problem head-on in 1931. In The Guardian of the Constitution, Schmitt argued that the guardian should be the president, elected directly by the people, because a parliament split into parties and a bench of judges could not represent the will of the people as a whole; his instrument was the emergency powers of Article 48 of the Weimar Constitution. Kelsen answered the same year that those who hold power cannot guard the limits of their own power, and named a constitutional court, set apart from politics, as guardian. Kelsen had designed the Austrian Constitutional Court in 1920.

History delivered a verdict. Schmitt's road ran through rule by emergency decree to Hitler's seizure of power in 1933. Postwar Germany took Kelsen's road with the Federal Constitutional Court in 1951, and Korea's Constitutional Court (1988) follows the Kelsen model too.

The debate is reopening because Kelsen's answer insulated only the exit. Guaranteed terms set justices apart from politics, but the entrance, their appointment, is still held by elected power. Once the electoral filter measures the power to mobilize, Kelsen's court takes in Schmittian force — the will of a mobilized majority — through the entrance. The question of who guards the guardians runs into mobilized politics and stops there. The remaining fix is to leave the removal switch and the term insulation as they are and repair the selection filter at the entrance.

Both halves of this piece close on the same structure. In a person, the brain stays original and the body is replaced, and the input cell sets the quality of the mind. In a society, the scale of law stays fixed and rulers are replaced, and the selection entrance sets the quality of power. In both, the core stays original, the edges are replaced, and the entrance is filtered.
