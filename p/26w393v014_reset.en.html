Once I automated pinning the site to the Wayback Machine, the archive's crawler started showing up on my visit counter. I put in a guard to keep its visits out of the count from now on, but picking out just the jump from the first round of pinning, one visit per page, was more trouble than it was worth, so I simply reset the counter to zero.

The crawler doesn't come in from one fixed IP, so ignoring an IP wasn't an option. What did show up was a pattern: it opens headless Chrome from California with a Linux header. Since hardly any human visitor is likely to arrive with a Linux header anyway, I just stopped counting Linux-header visits altogether.
