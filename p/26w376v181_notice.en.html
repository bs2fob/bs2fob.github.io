What the next stretch looks like

The site is still being built. The index, the page format, the illustration specs and the feeds are being settled one at a time, so the layout may change more than once over the next few days.

Pieces will come from older drafts rather than from new writing. Selecting what still holds up and reworking it is the main task for a while, which runs three months at the short end and a year at the long one. Very little written entirely from scratch will appear in that stretch.

Nothing is reposted as it stood. Each piece is judged again from where I am now and the sentences are rewritten before it goes up. The original date stays inside the piece.
