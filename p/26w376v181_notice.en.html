What the next stretch looks like

26w376v181_0912 Bs2FoB

The site is up and the setup is still being sorted out. The index, the page format, the illustration spec and the feed are getting settled one at a time, and while that goes on the layout may shift a few times over a few days.

The subject of the main series is already decided, and rather than write everything from scratch I plan to split older drafts and work them into short pieces. What I have accumulated over the years gets sifted for whatever still holds up by my current standards, reworked to fit the posting format, and put up — that is the bulk of it. This part alone will take three months at the short end and over a year at the long end. During that stretch I do not intend to attempt anything wholly new.

When an original goes up without much revision, I will stamp it with the date the original was written.
