In rock climbing —
you drive a piton, clip the rope, and climb
you climb, drive another piton, clip again
and climb again
and when you fall, you fall only to the last piton
and the rope carries you back up far more easily

1. This is a piece about procedure

The passage above was made in 2009. It was written straight out, whatever came to mind at the moment, and reading it again it shows a procedure for deciding what unit of work gets cut off and stacked.

An established foothold is a temporary fixed point the climber drives in while working up a steep face, and when an attempt past that point runs into trouble and goes wrong, it keeps the fall from going all the way down to the ground at the start, stopping it at the secured temporary fixed point.

The same principle applies, unchanged, to every kind of work a person does that cannot be finished in one breath and requires continuous, cumulative progress. If you do not secure how far things are settled with temporary footholds along the way, then when progress goes wrong you either start completely over or fall back to some half-remembered point, and short of that you keep repeating the same failure.

That half-remembered point is also an implicit foothold, not secured explicitly but found by groping through memory, and it is in several respects less efficient and less favorable than placing explicit footholds along the way. Emptying your memory and giving full attention to the progress itself is hardly comparable between the two.

2. The Bs and the Fob in Bs2Fob

The author of this site writes under the name Bs2Fob, where Bs abbreviates Base and Basic at once. Tabula rasa — the starting point emptied of all perception and cognition, which Locke figured as a blank slate — is the first Bs. It is the point from which every examination is stacked up, presupposing nothing already known, built only from observation, from abduction based on those observations, and from the verification and confirmation of that abduction.

Fob is the military abbreviation for forward operating base. It is taken as-is from a known usage, referred to simply as Fob in games and elsewhere. And this is what corresponds, first of all, to the established foothold drawn out of the figure above.

What separates the two is whether anything has been settled. BS holds nothing while FoB holds results that passed verification. A task, in its main body, is the work of setting out from BS and establishing a FoB.

3. An FoB becomes the next cycle's BS

And the structure above circulates in a spiral, turn after turn. A stabilized FoB becomes the BS of the next cycle, and from that Bs the next Fob is connected and built. A foothold once established is not a settled destination but a new starting point from which, for everything past it, you empty your perception again and carry the work on. The foothold secured this time becomes the floor the next examination stands on, and the results pass forward as material that reduces the next round of trial and error, in a structure recycled and raised without limit.

This is what the 2 in Bs2FoB does. The 2 is a connective abbreviation mimicking the sound of the English "to". Bs2FoB as a whole is not talking about two points; it is a pen name describing the movement of one point passing over into another.

4. A piece about finishing and perfecting

The passage above newly defines a term and usage, "finishing", set apart from the common term and usage "perfection", and uses it toward the close. Perfection names a state with nothing left to fix. Finishing, set apart from perfection, was defined as the verdict that secures the best available at this point and takes your hands off. The core of the piece is that as long as you aim at perfection the work does not end and no foothold is established.

So finishing comes to have a structure that begins by cutting off the compulsion toward perfection. The cut comes first and the securing second, and reversing the order defers the cut forever. You settle what can be settled, then state what you secured and what remains before moving on, and recording what is left in a form that can be referred to belongs to the work of securing.

5. Signs that no foothold is being established

If you are stuck on one problem, attempting the same spot over and over, you are not establishing footholds. It is not that the attempts failed; it is that no point to return to on failure was built, so every time you climb the same distance again.

6. What remains

The account of topping out, pulling the rope free, and finding the pitons driven along the way you climbed, that arrangement being the record itself, is after all a figurative account premised on a single fixed, attainable long-term goal. Even so, in that case it is not wrong to say that looking back from the destination over the secured footholds, where you passed to get here remains in them and can be checked and reviewed again.

But living itself in reality is closer to a journey from zero toward infinity with no point of arrival, and in the large sense arrival is the halting of progress, another word for death. There are many memeplex cults dressing this up as salvation or enlightenment and setting up shop with it, but from this writer's point of view they are observed as nothing more than the games of cute kindergarteners, nonsense delusion-play role-games.

※ A note on fertilizer

Human history calls the last 200 to 300 years the age of the modern scientific revolution, and the technological innovation achieved in that span, along with the unfolding of a human civilization that has all but plowed over the entire surface of the planet, overwhelms every result of the preceding fifty thousand years — or of the roughly ten thousand years in which anything worth calling civilization is found. At the root of this phenomenon lies the Bs2Fob approach written above, systematized as collective thought. This part will be examined properly again later, but it is the result of an explosion in the depth of examination, produced by the founding of the university as an educational and research institution in tandem with the printing and publishing revolution, and by the introduction and use of the journal system that shares, peer-reviews and verifies research results.

Counting only the number of modern humans who appeared on earth during this span, it exceeds the entire population of the preceding fifty thousand years, and this too connects to the discovery of the Haber-Bosch process, one of the results of the scientific and technological revolution. The roughly fifty years just before the discovery of Haber-Bosch had what is called the guano revolution, and the exhaustion of guano led to fixing atmospheric nitrogen directly through a chemical process and putting it into the soil, which served to break the cycle of unavoidable nitrogen exhaustion in cropland, the great crop failures and famine it brought, and the unavoidable conflict, slaughter and population control that followed. As of September 2026 the world population is about 8,316,680,000.

The United Nations began its official systematic world population statistics and projections (World Population Prospects) in 1950, and the world population figures at ten-year intervals from 1950 (medium projection and official estimate basis) are as follows.

1950: about 2,556,000,000
1960: about 3,039,000,000
1970: about 3,700,660,000
1980: about 4,453,000,000
1990: about 5,278,000,000
2000: about 6,082,000,000
2010: about 6,956,000,000
2020: about 7,794,000,000

The estimated world population as of 1500 is around 500 million, a level fairly recovered after the Black Death. The 1600 figure is estimated at under 600 million, and the 1700 figure was roughly under 700 million. The increase begins in earnest with the under-one-billion band around 1800 and the under-1.7-billion band in 1900, and the 1900 figure was achieved as nitrogen exhaustion in cropland was held off, after a fashion, by the limited use of biological sediment through the guano revolution. After 1900 comes the age of Haber-Bosch in earnest, and by the early 1960s the figure has already passed twice the 1900 level.
