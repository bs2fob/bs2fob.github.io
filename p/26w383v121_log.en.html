Rewriting 001, and a few settings

26w383v121_0916 Bs2FoB

The tone of the posted piece was too flippant, so I rewrote it from scratch. The frame stayed as it was, and a section on fertilizer plus its illustration went in at the end. I set justified text as the default, since that reads cleanest as a layout. In the earlier version the font came out far too large in mobile view and the flow of the writing got mashed up, so the mobile layout settings were reworked as well. This tab started out as [Notice]; it is now tagged [Log], since what it actually holds is working notes.

The idea behind the series as a whole has firmed up quite a bit, and the first piece of it is the Cognitive Axioms ### numbering. There are slots from 001 to 999. I started with two digits, then widened it, on the judgment that short pieces linked to each other beat one long sprawling piece. The spine of what goes in there is already settled: the four-part Lit-Living Filtering written in February 2026, and the recent two-part Cognitive Axioms. In order of exposition the axioms come first and the filtering stacks on top of them. The Three-Way Split of Intelligence, the Speech Pattern Review, and a few other short pieces have each earned a share that cannot be cut. What remains is how to arrange them, one at a time, and roll them out.
