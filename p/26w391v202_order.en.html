Reordering the Lists

26w391v202_0921 Bs2FoB

First, I changed the Writing tab so that Cognitive Axioms 01_0 shows up at the front of the list. The pieces stack up in order, like Lego bricks, the Bs2FoB way — so if the way in is newest-first, that is, backwards, it can add noise to how people come in. Going by weight alone, an earlier piece isn't twice as heavy as a later one; it's something like a million times heavier. In fact, nothing I write afterward can offset the weight of 01_0.

While I was at it, I changed the Verse tab to the same order. The one sentence that has run through all my work for the last eighteen years or so is a line from a Rainer Maria Rilke novel: "Writing — that will be the end of everything." From it came the basic form of my writing, "capture the scene – attach a tag – connect the narrative – refer back later," and as that routine piled up in a spiral, the Cognitive Axioms series appeared.

The verses are my own kind of memo format, tuned for referring back later, which sits somewhere between attaching a tag and connecting the narrative. Through a three-step reset of <Delta–Lima–Junk>, a recognition fires and settles, gets embodied and forgotten, gets recognized again, and after that turns into a reflexive condition — something so obvious there's no need to pull it out and look at it, the way a bird doesn't notice air or a fish doesn't notice water. The verses are a device that speeds up that process. Some are heavy, some are light, some are off-the-cuff and barely matter. The light ones I might post and take down, but the heavy early ones are more or less fixed, so they follow the same order as the Writing tab.

I reworked one of my older texts into a new chunk of writing, but it's on hold for now. It will be numbered 05_#, and a standalone piece split off from the original as well. The original frame broke down into five parts in all. Still, it feels like the conditions for entering the main body of the "Cognitive Axioms" haven't even been set up yet. I may leave the 05 series unposted and poke at the Lexicon tab. That tab is a branch that needs fairly involved design, so it's a bit of a nuisance. For a start, I'm stuck on how many slots to give it.

Anyway, resetting the list order is a new setting I'm fairly happy with. That this sort of thing isn't visible from the start is common to all work. Once you start and keep running, observations about what to do and how to do it appear. It reflects the loop in which moving forward is itself what opens up the possibility of observing.
