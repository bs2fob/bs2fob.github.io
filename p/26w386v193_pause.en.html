Up to 007 Without Much Thought

26w386v193_0919 Bs2FoB

I had no plans to write anything today. But the intelligence trichotomy holds a lot of the basic definitions, so it felt like a reference point other pieces would lean on, and I thought, why not write it up as <Cognitive Axioms 002>? Then, when I went to post it, the chunk felt too big, so I split it into three steps: 002, 003, and 004. The original text already existed; what I wrote this time blends that original with the better arguments that came after it. The series itself actually started when the two-part Cognitive Axioms essay was finished. That work made many of my shaky footholds a good deal sturdier, so the older pieces were bound to need revising anyway.

Once 004 was up, I thought, might as well go a bit further? So I carried on to 007. The material is parts 1 and 2 of the switched-on living cognizer filtering. I went as far as the living cognizer and its default settings, and haven't touched the active and dormant living cognizer yet. The plan for that part isn't settled, there's no need to settle it right now, and so it's on hold.

To talk about the active living cognizer, you first need to understand the circuits of automatic thought that lock in the dormant state — the bias systems that act as heavy noise on active, deliberate cognition. Then memeplexes have to come in, then how the scheme of Plato's theory of Forms played out, and the Malthusian trap that underlies exclusive collectivism needs a mention too. Memeplexes are essentially parasitic on exclusive collectivism, and the two keep feeding off each other, so this is no simple matter.

The Russia–Ukraine war. The 2026 armed clash between Iran and the United States. Events right in front of us connect directly to this, which makes it sensitive. With so many people still following belief systems, some with official names and organizations and some only implicit, taking on an argument that breaks apart the very idea of a belief system is not something to do lightly. To talk about memeplexes you have to deal with memes, then genes, then the genetics that knocked down the inheritance of acquired characteristics, and then weigh the damage, or the supplement, that recent epigenetics has brought to the DNA-determinist frame.

So for now I don't plan to move straight on to the active living cognizer. For a while, maybe, the next numbers may go off in an entirely different direction. There are plenty of candidates, and the test is the procedural view of Bs2FoB: how often will this piece be referred back to as footing by the numbers that follow? Up to the living cognizer the issues are fairly basic, but there are already parts, like attractors and Hamilton, that don't appear in any paper, and parts where I took a provocative, little-noticed hypothesis like MEP at face value.

If the writing goes the way I intend, many theories that have been handled with unquestioned weight will get cut down — argued to be false, or accepted only within drawn limits. From the author's side, each of those is a task that takes some hard checking and review, so it's a bit of a nuisance.

I'm just laying out my own thinking on a blank page, breaking the walls I run into along the way and using them as stepping stones. My writing has never set out from someone else's account as a reference point in order to work its way toward understanding it.

I've seen what monsters Socrates and Aristotle were, and those walls became the stepping stones from which the cognitive axioms emerged. The monster now taking shape, around the unavoidable subjects of axis coordinate systems, particle physics, and electromagnetic repulsion, is Einstein. In Park Moon-ho's spin lecture, the second post in the Snap tab, he takes a stance that doesn't question at root the particle physics that science has built up under the authority of Nobel prizes. Even so, he barely mentions fractional dimensions or topology, which suggests he isn't entirely without a concern like mine. My position is that the concept of an axis coordinate system itself is not yet clearly established. From where I stand, nonsense like twelve-dimensional brane theory has a hard time even getting started, and the coordinate system of a time axis and space axes that the Lorentz transformation of special relativity takes as its premise doesn't hold as a given either; I see it as a kind of trick. My explanation of relativity stays fixed on Feynman's way of doing it with only three coordinate axes.

I just added a few more lines while I was at it. I don't count this as a piece of writing in particular; it's only an addendum to the notice that, with today's run to 007, progress will pause again for a while.
