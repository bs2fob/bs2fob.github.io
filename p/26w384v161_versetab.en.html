A Verse Tab

A Verse tab went in between Log and Writing, and the pieces that were lying around in plain sight went up with it. The numbering is ####, which leaves 9999 slots. These usually come out as 4+3=7 characters across four lines, built by fitting Korean words together until they sit right. On a good day more than ten of them show up, so the numbering needed room.

0001 carries an attack on the time axis — the one that has proved itself over and over as a filter for detecting nonsense. It is a long way from seize the day and carpe diem. It simply argues that a time that is not now and a place that is not here cannot hold. Every lie starts by routing around that argument.

No need to go deeper than that. Just a few words while the log was open.
